title: AI patents and US consumer spending
summary: "BA dissertation: a difference-in-differences study of AI patenting and consumer spending across 51 US states, 1998 to 2020."
problem: Does a rise in AI patenting change what households spend their money on?
role: Solo project; analysis in Stata.
tracks: [data]
stack: [Stata, Difference-in-differences, Econometrics]
result: No significant effect. Food came closest (p = 0.054) and moved to p = 0.039 with a different lag structure, so the result depends on timing. Parallel trends held for food and transport but failed for apparel; robustness checks done.
